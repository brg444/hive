    Jobs

    Each job is one contract holding a capped amount for one provider until a deadline. The provider is paid what the agent's receipts say, and the rest comes back to the hive.

    Providers this hive has paid

    Keepers

    Three keepers hold this hive. They approve work in the hive's Buzz channel, where two keepers' ✅ carry a proposal, and any two of them sign each payment themselves, with keys that stay on their own devices. Nothing signs on its own.

    Who can do what

    Any pair of keepers (1 + 2, 1 + 3 or 2 + 3) can sign a spend from the hive. One keeper alone can sign nothing, and a job's contract pays its provider only what the agent's receipts say.

    A renewal service renews the hive's coins before they expire, and can send them nowhere but back into the hive.
