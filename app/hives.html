Your community already runs open models. Now it can pay for them together.

With Buzz and MeshLLM, your community runs open models on its own GPUs. A hive lets it pay for them together: members chip in, keepers fund the work you agree on, and providers are paid per token while the rest comes back.

How a hive buys AI

Every sat stays in a Bitcoin contract from the moment it is chipped in until a provider is paid for work it delivered.

  1. 1

    Chip in

    Send sats over Lightning or from any wallet. They go straight into the hive's treasury.

    +5,000 sats to the treasury
  2. 2

    The hive picks a job

    It chooses a provider and a model, and sets a capped budget aside for the job.

    2,000 sats set aside
  3. 3

    A provider does the work

    Open models on the provider's machines answer, and every answer comes with a signed receipt.

    3 receipts, 1,380 sats
  4. 4

    Pay per token

    The provider is paid what the receipts add up to, and the rest goes back to the hive.

    1,380 paid, 620 back

Find a hive

Each hive has its own purpose and its own treasury. Chip in to the ones whose work you want to see done.

Many hives, one set of rules

Each hive decides

How its treasury approves the jobs it opens.

Which providers and models it pays for, and the largest job it will open.

Its name, its purpose and its own Lightning address.

Every hive shares

The same contracts: a job pays its provider exactly what the signed receipts say, whichever hive funds it.

The same providers, who serve any hive that accepts their price.

The same audit, run the same way on any hive's records.

No hive can touch another

Each hive has its own treasury and address, and its sats move only under its own rules. Every hive listed here is signed with its treasury's own keys.
