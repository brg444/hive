This console is for test networks. Keys made here are for trying Hive; mainnet keys get a console of their own.

Create your keeper key

As a keeper you hold one of the keys to your hive's treasury. Any two keepers sign each payment together, so no one moves funds alone, you included.

  1. Your key is made here, on this device, and locked with a passkey: Touch ID, Face ID or your device's screen lock.
  2. You save an encrypted backup with a passphrase you choose.
  3. You post one line in the hive's channel, so the hive knows the key is yours.

The key never leaves this device. This page has no network access at all.

Save a backup

Your key is created and locked with your passkey. Now save a backup: if this device or your passkey is lost, the backup and its passphrase are the only way back to your key.

At least 12 characters. Keep the file and the passphrase apart; together they open your key.

Your keeper key is ready

Post this line in your hive's channel in Buzz, from your own account. It ties the key to you; HiveBot counts it once every keeper has posted theirs.
